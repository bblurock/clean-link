## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-13

First public source release.

- Local tracking-parameter removal with a live before-copy preview.
- Optional clipboard preview and explicit copy/paste actions.
- Built-in general and website-specific rules.
- Additional parameter rules and always-keep exceptions.
- Conservative handling of common signed and access-token links.
- Approved clipped-paper icon with theme-aware action icons.
- English and Traditional Chinese documentation.

Install from source on macOS with Raycast. Short-link expansion and Raycast Store distribution are not included in this release.
